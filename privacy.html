Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 18, 2026

Gravity Graffiti respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and processed when you use the Gravity Graffiti mobile game.

Information We Collect

Gravity Graffiti does not require you to create an account or provide your name, email address, or other personal profile information in order to play.

Gameplay information such as level progress, Lives, Sparks, unlocked cosmetics, settings, achievements, and scores may be stored locally on your device so your progress can be maintained between sessions.

Advertising

Gravity Graffiti may use Google AdMob to provide optional rewarded advertisements. Ads are not automatically shown during normal gameplay. Players choose whether to watch a rewarded advertisement in exchange for an optional in-game benefit.

Google and its advertising partners may automatically process certain information when an advertisement is requested or displayed. This may include device information, IP address, advertising identifiers, advertisement interactions, diagnostic information, and performance data.

Advertising behavior may vary depending on your device settings, geographic location, consent choices, and applicable privacy laws.

In-App Purchases

Gravity Graffiti may offer optional in-app purchases, including Unlimited Lives. Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. Gravity Graffiti does not directly collect or store your payment card information.

Tracking and Advertising Choices

Where required, Gravity Graffiti may ask for consent before certain advertising technologies are used. You may also have privacy and advertising controls available through your Apple device settings.

Children's Privacy

Gravity Graffiti does not knowingly request personal information directly from children. Advertising and privacy settings will be configured in accordance with applicable platform requirements and privacy laws.

Third-Party Services

Gravity Graffiti may rely on third-party services necessary to provide features such as advertisements and in-app purchases. These services operate according to their own privacy policies.

Third-party services may include:

  • Google AdMob
  • Apple App Store and StoreKit

Data Retention

Gameplay data stored locally remains on your device until it is removed by the app, reset by the player, or deleted when the app is removed, depending on the type of data and system behavior.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Gravity Graffiti gains new features or services. Any updated policy will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

Contact

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or Gravity Graffiti, please visit our Support page.
