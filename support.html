Gravity Graffiti Support

Need help with Gravity Graffiti? We're here to help.

Gameplay

Draw paths to guide falling confetti into the collection hole. Complete levels, earn Stars and Sparks, unlock cosmetics, and work toward Perfect Drops.

Lives

Lives are used when beginning a level attempt. If you successfully pass a level, the Lives used for that attempt are returned.

Lives can also regenerate over time. Optional rewarded ads may provide additional Lives.

Rewarded Ads

Gravity Graffiti does not use forced advertisements. Rewarded ads are optional and only appear when you choose to watch one in exchange for an in-game reward.

In-App Purchases

Gravity Graffiti may offer an optional Unlimited Lives purchase through the Apple App Store.

If you previously purchased Unlimited Lives and it is not appearing, use the Restore Purchases option inside the game's Settings menu.

App Issues

If you experience a bug, crash, purchase issue, or gameplay problem, please include your iPhone model, iOS version, and a short description of what happened when contacting support.
